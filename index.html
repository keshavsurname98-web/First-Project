    How to play

    Launch. Press anywhere away from the star and drag in the direction you want the planet to travel. A longer drag gives it more speed. Click or tap for a launch with orbital speed.

    Experiment. Try a different starting system or change the speed to see how each path develops. With a keyboard, focus the field, move the crosshair with the arrow keys, and press Enter to launch at orbital speed. Press Space in the field to pause or resume.

    Flight School. Drag from the launch pad to set your heading and speed. Guide the probe through the numbered checkpoints in order. With the field focused, use Left or Right to change heading, Up or Down to change speed, and Enter to launch.
